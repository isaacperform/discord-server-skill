## 👋 Welcome to the closed beta

We're opening early to a small group before launch, so expect rough edges. That's the point: what you try and tell us here becomes the next update.

### 📍 How to start
1. **Open the app** → {{app}}
2. {{#updates}} — what changed, every release.
3. {{#bugs}} — anything broken or confusing? Post it. One screenshot is enough.
4. {{#ideas}} — features you wish existed. We look at the most 👍 first.
5. {{#qna}} — how-to questions. Answers pile up into our FAQ.
6. {{#lounge}} — come say hi!

### 🤝 Three promises
- Please ask before posting beta screens outside this server.
- Be specific in reports: where → what you did → what you saw.
- What you see about other members stays here.

Can't see the beta channels? DM the team.
