## 👋 Welcome in!

This is where people who share an interest ask, share and show off. Learn more → {{site}}

### 📍 How to start
1. {{#introductions}} — tell us what you do in a line or two.
2. {{#qna}} — stuck? Ask away. Answers become everyone's FAQ.
3. {{#resources}} — guides and templates collected by the team.
4. {{#showcase}} — show what you made or achieved.
5. {{#notice}} — events and news land here.

### 🤝 Three rules
- Be kind. Harassment, ads and spam get removed without warning.
- Ask specific questions: situation + what you tried + what you want.
- Resources here are for your own use. Ask before sharing them elsewhere.
